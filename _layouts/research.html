ABOUT ME

Efficient systems.
Better learning.

I am an undergraduate in the Yao Class at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University.

I am currently a research intern at MIT Han Lab, advised by Prof. Song Han. I also work with Prof. Wei Xu at Tsinghua on distributed systems for large language model training.

My research focuses on machine learning systems: making large-model training and inference more efficient and reliable, and improving how language models learn to reason.

01 / REINFORCEMENT LEARNINGPreprint · 2026

Hide to GuideLearning via Semantic Masking

Ruitao Liu · Co-first author · MIT Han Lab

Expert reasoningPlan + reward-relevant content
→
Semantic maskingKeep the plan · mask the answer
→
Policy learningReconstruct the missing steps

Developed Semantic Masked Expert Policy Optimization (SMEPO) for expert-guided reinforcement learning. The method preserves useful reasoning structure while masking information that could let a model copy its way to a reward.

Evaluated on mathematical reasoning, code, and agent tasks.

02 / DISTRIBUTED TRAININGPreprint · 2026

A Readiness-Driven RuntimePipeline-Parallel Training under Runtime Variability

Ruitao Liu · First author · Advisor: Prof. Wei Xu

Designed and implemented a runtime that dispatches ready microbatch work in a Megatron-based training framework. Existing schedules provide priorities, while actual dependencies determine what can execute.

Dynamic pipeline execution that adapts to computation and communication variability.
